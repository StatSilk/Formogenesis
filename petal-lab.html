Experimental petal comparison · growth mechanics preview

Compare a procedural reference (orange) with a reduced-order metric fit (green). Center-versus-edge growth drives cupping and ripple selection; an upper–lower strain mismatch drives bilayer curl. Heatmap mode still compares curvature estimators on identical reference geometry.

Scientific status: Green minimizes a growth-metric mismatch over a small family of candidate cup/ripple surfaces with bending and core-restraint penalties; the ripple mode is informed by a strip-instability scale, rather than fitted from botanical scans. This is not a full nonlinear thin-shell equilibrium or cellular-growth simulation. Bilayer curvature uses the equal-thickness, equal-modulus strip approximation κ = 3Δε/(2h), with assumed millimetre dimensions and bounded total rotation. The model is qualitative, uncalibrated and does not solve collisions. Heatmap mode compares two independently normalized curvature proxies on identical geometry; colors are not equal physical units.
