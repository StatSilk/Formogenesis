Botanical explanation

Experimental model. Flower forms and bloom animations are illustrative approximations, not mathematically exact reconstructions or predictive plant biology. The inhibition-radius experiment uses a simplified phenomenological field.

The model combines artistic geometry with limited growth-metric and bilayer approximations; it is not a predictive plant-growth or full elastic-sheet simulation. Spiral phyllotaxis can emerge in packed florets on composite flower heads; flowers with few petals commonly exhibit whorl symmetry.
